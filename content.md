### Why Work With Us

- **Flexible Shifts:** Choose working hours that suit your schedule and lifestyle.
- **Competitive Pay:** Transparent rates with prompt, reliable payroll schedules.
- **Professional Development:** Access to ongoing training, safeguarding updates, and career pathways.
- **Dedicated Support Team:** 24/7 Admin support to ensure safe and organized placements.

### Who We Are Hiring (Role List)

- Interim Managers
- Registered Nurses
- 1:1 Support Workers & Care Assistants
- Chefs, Assistant Cooks & Kitchen Assistants
- Housekeeping, Cleaners & Housing Assistants

### Application Process (Step-by-Step)

1. **Submit Application:** Fill out the online registration form.
2. **Compliance & Vetting:** Document verification (DBS, Right to Work, references).
3. **Onboarding & Induction:** Briefing on New Era standards and safety protocols.
4. **Start Working:** Receive shift bookings that match your skills and availability.

### Application Form Fields

- Full Name, Email, Phone Number
- Target Role(s) Selected via Checkbox
- Upload CV / Resume (`.pdf`, `.docx`)
- Current Availability (Full-Time, Part-Time, Weekends, Nights)
